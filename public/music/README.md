# LeanQuest music

Two MIDI files are generated by `scripts/generate-music.mjs`:

- `title.mid` — William Byrd's “Ave Verum Corpus,” arranged for four synthesized voices using the [public-domain 1921 Oxford University Press score](https://imslp.org/wiki/Ave_verum_corpus_(Byrd,_William)) (IMSLP #232546) as the reference. Note data was checked against [Monique Rio's CC BY 4.0 edition and MIDI](https://stcpress.org/pieces/ave_verum_corpus). This adaptation performs the score's repeat but omits the Amen: alto, tenor, and bass release halfway through the final measure while soprano sustains to the loop boundary.
- `story-induction.mid` — J. S. Bach's “Gib dich zufrieden und sei stille,” BWV 511, adapted from [Mutopia Project music ID 1009](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=1009). The source melody and continuo are public domain. This soft chiptune-organ arrangement adds a quiet open-fifth bourdon and writes two complete passes into the MIDI before looping.

The underlying Byrd and Bach compositions used by the story music are public domain. To the extent LeanQuest contributors hold any copyright or related rights in the MIDI adaptations, those rights are waived under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); see [`LICENSE-STORY.md`](./LICENSE-STORY.md). The notice is embedded in each story MIDI as a Copyright Notice meta-event. Run `npm run music:license` to restore this metadata, as well as the combat-music metadata, if a file is replaced.

`battle.mid` is Susan Petcher's “Battle Moosak,” the active looping battle cue. `victory.mid` is her “Triumph” cue. Both works are copyright © 2026 Susan Petcher and licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/); see [`LICENSE-COMBAT.md`](./LICENSE-COMBAT.md) for the applicable files and suggested attribution. LeanQuest adapts the works with custom chiptune synthesis and synchronization to interactive audiovisual sequences. Each title and license notice is also embedded in its MIDI metadata.

The browser renders every MIDI through Tone.js synthesizers rather than relying on platform MIDI instruments.

`story-rescue-fugue.mid` is the fugue from J. S. Bach's “Great” Fantasia and Fugue in G minor, BWV 542, sourced from [Mutopia Project music ID 984](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=984). The score and MIDI source are public domain. The game preserves the complete fugue and renders its seven audible parts with a custom chiptune church-organ voice.

Both induction story paths use `story-induction.mid`.
