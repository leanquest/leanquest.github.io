# LeanQuest music

These five original MIDI compositions are generated by `scripts/generate-music.mjs`:

- `title.mid` — “Lanterns Above the Proof”
- `combat.mid` — “Theorem Under Siege”
- `victory.mid` — “Q.E.D. Fanfare”
- `story-broken-axiom.mid` — “Fracture in the First Theorem”
- `story-hall-of-names.mid` — “Names Without End”

They use an original retro-JRPG vocabulary and share a small descending motif, but do not reproduce melodies from an existing game. The browser renders the MIDI through Tone.js synthesizers rather than relying on platform MIDI instruments.

The active looping battle cue is `battle.mid`. The generated `combat.mid` remains an alternate composition and is not used by the game.
