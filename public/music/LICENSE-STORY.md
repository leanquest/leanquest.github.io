# LeanQuest story music notice

This notice applies to the following MIDI adaptations:

- `title.mid` — William Byrd, “Ave Verum Corpus”
- `story-induction.mid` — J. S. Bach, “Gib dich zufrieden und sei stille,” BWV 511
- `story-rescue-fugue.mid` — J. S. Bach, Fugue in G minor, BWV 542/2

The underlying compositions by William Byrd and J. S. Bach are in the public domain.

The LeanQuest adaptation of “Ave Verum Corpus” was independently transcribed from the public-domain 1921 Oxford University Press score. Its note data was checked against Monique Rio's edition, © Monique Rio, licensed under CC BY 4.0. LeanQuest performs the score's repeat, omits the Amen, and changes the final releases for looping playback.

To the extent LeanQuest contributors hold any copyright or related rights in these MIDI adaptations, those rights are waived under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The same notice is embedded in each MIDI as a Copyright Notice meta-event.
